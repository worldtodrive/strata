about

Strata sits on the boundary between cozy driving game and geospatial simulation with an ambitious goal: drive anywhere in the world at 1:1 scale using open data and procedural generation.

Here in this initial demo you can drive two levels rendered in the Dallas, TX area:

1. High Five Interchange (US-75 x I-635)
2. The Village neighborhood
